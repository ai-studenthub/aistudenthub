5 Free AI Tools to Improve Your Essay Writing

Good ideas can still lose marks if they're buried in a run-on sentence or a paragraph that never actually makes its point. These tools won't write the essay for you, but they'll catch the small things that make a real difference to a grader — clarity, tone, and structure.

1. Grammarly

The standard for a reason. Beyond catching typos, Grammarly's tone detector flags when a sentence reads as unclear or overly casual for an academic paper, which is often more useful than the grammar corrections themselves.

2. Hemingway Editor

Hemingway highlights sentences that are hard to read and calls out passive voice and unnecessary adverbs. Paste in a paragraph you're not happy with and it'll show you exactly where the sentence is doing too much at once.

3. ChatGPT (as an editor, not a writer)

Instead of asking ChatGPT to write your essay, paste in a paragraph you've already written and ask it to point out where your argument is weak or unclear. This keeps the ideas yours while still getting a second pair of eyes.

4. QuillBot

QuillBot's paraphrasing tool is useful when you know what you want to say but the sentence keeps coming out clunky. Type your original sentence in, see a few rephrased versions, and pick the one that actually sounds like you.

5. Wordtune

Wordtune suggests rewrites for a sentence at a time, with options to make it more formal, more concise, or more expansive. Good for polishing a thesis statement or a conclusion that isn't landing quite right.

A quick note on using these responsibly

These are editing tools, not ghostwriters. Using AI to generate your actual argument or analysis crosses into academic integrity territory at most universities — check your school's specific policy before relying on any tool beyond grammar and clarity checks.
