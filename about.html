about the project

A directory built between classes, not in a boardroom.

AI Student Hub started as a personal list of AI tools that actually held up under deadline pressure — the ones that survived being tested on a real assignment, not just a demo. It grew into a directory organized the way students actually think about their work: by task, not by product category.

What belongs here

Every tool listed has a free tier or is free to use outright. We link out to the tool itself rather than reproducing its content, and each entry says plainly what the tool is good for and where it tends to fall short.

What doesn't belong here

Tools that require payment with no usable free option, tools that primarily encourage submitting work you didn't do yourself, and anything that misrepresents what a university expects from original coursework.

How the site makes money

AI Student Hub is supported by advertising, which helps cover hosting costs. Ads are served by Google AdSense and are kept separate from the resource listings — nothing on this site is a paid placement disguised as a recommendation. See the Privacy Policy for details on how ads and analytics work here.

Who's behind it

AI Student Hub is an independent, student-run project. It isn't affiliated with any university or any of the tools listed in the directory.
