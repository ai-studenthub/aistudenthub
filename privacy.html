Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information AI Student Hub collects, how it's used, and the choices available to you. It applies to this website only.

Information we collect

AI Student Hub does not require an account and does not collect personal information beyond what you choose to send us — for example, through the contact form, which opens your own email client rather than submitting data to our servers.

Cookies and similar technologies

Like most websites that carry advertising, this site may use cookies and similar technologies set by us or by third-party services — for example, to remember your search or category filter preferences, or to support the advertising described below.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies allows it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits here and elsewhere.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

Third-party links

Articles on this site link out to external AI tools and services. Once you leave AI Student Hub, that site's own privacy policy applies — we don't control, and aren't responsible for, how third-party sites handle your data.

Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are useful, such as aggregate page-view counts. This data is not used to identify individual visitors.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for university students and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so may affect how ads are personalized but won't prevent you from using the site.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
