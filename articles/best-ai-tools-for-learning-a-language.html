5 Free AI Tools for Learning a New Language

Whether you're fulfilling a language requirement or picking up a new one for yourself, the hardest part is usually consistent practice — especially speaking practice, which is hard to get outside a classroom. These tools fill that gap in different ways.

1. Duolingo Max

Duolingo's AI-powered tier adds conversational practice scenarios and an "explain my answer" feature that breaks down exactly why a translation was right or wrong, going beyond the base app's simple correct/incorrect feedback.

2. ChatGPT (as a conversation partner)

Ask ChatGPT to have a conversation with you entirely in your target language, at a level appropriate for where you're at. It's patient in a way a real conversation partner might not be, and you can ask it to correct your grammar as you go.

3. Speak

Speak is built specifically around AI-powered speaking practice, giving you real-time feedback on pronunciation and fluency — the skill most language apps genuinely struggle to teach well.

4. DeepL

More accurate than most general translation tools for nuance and context, DeepL is useful for checking your own writing in the target language rather than just relying on literal word-for-word translation.

5. LingQ

LingQ uses AI to track which words you already know as you read or listen to real content in your target language, then highlights only the unfamiliar words — letting you learn vocabulary in context instead of from a flashcard deck.

A quick note on using these responsibly

AI conversation practice is a supplement, not a substitute for real interaction. If your course has a speaking component or conversation partner requirement, these tools work best as practice beforehand, not as a replacement for it.
