5 Free AI Tools to Help You Study Smarter

Rereading the same textbook chapter for the third time isn't studying — it's just rereading. These tools help turn passive review into active recall, which is what actually makes information stick before an exam.

1. Khanmigo

Khan Academy's AI tutor walks you through a problem step by step instead of just handing you the answer, using the Socratic method to help you find the reasoning yourself. Particularly useful for math and science courses where understanding the process matters more than the final number.

2. Quizlet (Q-Chat)

Quizlet's AI feature can generate practice questions from your own notes or a topic you type in, then quiz you conversationally instead of just flipping flashcards. Good for last-minute review sessions before a test.

3. Notion AI

If you already take notes in Notion, its built-in AI can summarize a long page of lecture notes into key points, or turn messy bullet points into a clean study outline in seconds.

4. Otter.ai

Otter transcribes lectures in real time and can summarize the recording afterward into key topics and action items. Useful for classes where the professor talks fast or covers a lot of ground in one session.

5. ChatGPT (for practice questions)

Paste in your lecture notes or textbook chapter and ask ChatGPT to generate practice questions based on the material. It's a fast way to test whether you actually understood something or just skimmed it.

A quick note on using these responsibly

These tools work best as a supplement to actually engaging with the material, not a replacement for it. Generating flashcards from notes you never read yourself will not help you on exam day.
