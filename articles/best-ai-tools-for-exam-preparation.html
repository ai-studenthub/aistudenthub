5 Free AI Tools to Prep for Exams Faster

Rereading a textbook chapter feels productive but rarely tells you what you actually don't know yet. These tools focus on active recall and practice testing — the study method backed by the most research on what actually improves retention.

1. Khanmigo

Khan Academy's AI tutor can generate practice problems on a specific topic and walk you through the reasoning when you get one wrong, instead of just marking it incorrect and moving on.

2. Quizlet (Q-Chat)

Turn your own notes or flashcard set into a quiz Quizlet's AI conducts conversationally, adapting the difficulty based on what you're getting right or wrong as you go.

3. ChatGPT (for practice exams)

Paste in your course material or textbook chapter and ask for a set of exam-style questions, including a mix of easy and hard ones. This is one of the fastest ways to find out what you actually don't understand yet.

4. Anki with AI add-ons

Anki's spaced repetition system is already proven to work well for long-term retention; several community AI add-ons can auto-generate flashcards from a PDF or your notes, cutting down the tedious card-making step.

5. Photomath

For STEM exams specifically, Photomath scans a problem and walks through the full solution step by step, which is more useful for exam prep than just getting the final answer — you can see exactly where your own approach diverges.

A quick note on using these responsibly

Practice questions are only useful if you actually attempt them yourself first. Reading through AI-generated answers without trying the problem cold defeats the purpose of practice testing.
